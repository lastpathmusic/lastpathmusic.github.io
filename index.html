About

Last Path is the solo live-looping project of Steve Yang, blending controllerism, layered vocals, and live instrumentation to build songs in real time.

Performances range from two distinct vibes: unplugged, with a more organic, intimate feel centered on guitar, vocals, and subtle looping; and chill electronic sets, blending live instrumentation, synths, and beats into a relaxed, atmospheric backdrop.

Perfect for: Cafes, patios, lounges, and private events.
